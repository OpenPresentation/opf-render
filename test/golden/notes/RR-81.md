# RR-81: OPF 0.19 layout templates

**Example corpus: no hash moves.** Against core opf#577 (`codex/rr-79-layouts-core` at bb42a314), `node test/golden.mjs`
passes the default baseline (`opf-examples-png.cover-centering`) unchanged, 807 slides in 127 decks, with and without this
change. The 0.18 records (`opf-layout/v1`) compose byte-for-byte in core until RR-80/RR-83 swap the default catalog, and
the renderer change only acts on what template slides carry (`item.listColumns`, `item.region`).

**New baseline: `test/golden/layout-templates-0.19.json`** (`test/layout-template-goldens.mjs`), 346 slides:

- the 28 built-in templates (core's design-doc fixture, `test/fixtures/layout-templates-0.19.json`) with 1 to 6 blocks of
  short and of long content, the sample content of core's composition goldens (`template-goldens.test.mjs`), so the two
  goldens describe the same slides; even counts set `design.contentBox`, so carded and plain regions are both covered;
- 10 feature slides: lists in two and three columns (numbered, bulleted, described, carded with lower-roman numbers, and a
  payload's fixed `columns: 3`), bled `cover` and `image` regions on carded slides, and `cover`, `image-beside` and `hero`
  mirrored (deck `design.mirror`).

Each entry is the SHA-256 of the traced SVG (so region groups and list-column groups are pinned too) and of resvg's PNG at
scale 0.25 with bundled fonts and estimated measurement. Pictures are embedded SVG data URIs, so bleed and crops show.

Generated on Windows (Node 24.21.0) against the core above; the same machine reproduces the example-corpus baseline
byte-for-byte, and CI (the pinned Linux Playwright image) confirms these hashes on the PR. All 346 slides were reviewed on
the 15 review sheets `--update` writes. What the review checked: bled pictures reach the slide edges and never get a card
(cover, image, mirrored cover), cards sit where core composed them, and every list in columns shows all its items with
numbering continuing (1 to 16 over two columns, i to xviii over two, three-column bullets and described FAQ entries).

What the review saw that is core's composition, not the renderer, and is left to core (opf#577) rather than changed here:
blocks a template has no room for are drawn in core's implicit row below the grid, which on a few slides (for example
`hero 5 long`, `hero 6 long`, `section 5 long`) is too short for its text, so lines overlap at the minimum size; when a
supporting row and extra blocks squeeze the primary region (`timeline 5 long`, `timeline 6 long`, `table-beside 5 long`,
`table-beside 6 long`, `comparison 6 long`), its content is drawn past its cell, up into the title; and overflowing column
regions (`agenda 5 long`, `agenda 6 long`, `list 5 long`, `faq 6 long`) draw their text past the region, as any overflow
does.

Regenerate with `node test/layout-template-goldens.mjs --update` (candidate, changed slides and review sheets under
`artifacts/template-goldens/`), review, then `--promote`, and explain the change in a note here.
