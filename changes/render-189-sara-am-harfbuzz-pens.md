opf-render#189 (output-changing for PNG and raster PDF of Indic, Thai, Lao, Khmer and Myanmar text when the conversion has HarfBuzz, which the Node fonts handle and the bundled faces always have): the raster path pins each complex-script cluster at HarfBuzz's pen (`fonts.outlines.shape`, the browser's shaper) instead of fontkit's, falling back to fontkit's pens without a shaper (output then unchanged) or for a run HarfBuzz cannot map back to its characters. fontkit kerns after a decomposed Thai sara am differently, so a cluster after it (ใจ after น้ำ) was about 0.7 px off at 25 px. The script corpora's `thai-title`, `thai-sara-am`, `thai-no-spaces`, `taml-title`, `telu-title` and `mymr-medials` now draw exactly as HarfBuzz's outlines (mean pixel difference 0.46, 1.48, 0.47, 5.7, 2.79 and 12.96 before); `thai-sara-am` at 60 px against Chromium goes from 266 channel values off by more than 64 (max 255) to 43 (max 170), the anti-aliasing difference HarfBuzz's own outlines show too. The approved raster goldens do not change.
